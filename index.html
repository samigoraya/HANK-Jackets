Questions

Do you ship outside Pakistan?

No. We deliver only within Pakistan.

Is shipping really free?

Yes. Shipping is free on every order to any city in Pakistan.

Can I pay on delivery?

Yes. Cash on Delivery works in every city we deliver to.

How long does delivery take?

3–5 working days for most cities. Remote areas can take 1–2 extra days.

What if the size is off?

Swap or return it within 7 days, unworn and with tags.

How do I pick my size?

Open the size guide on any jacket page. It lists chest and length in inches for S to XXL.
